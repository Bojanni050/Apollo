The working folder, chosen in the wizard and shown afterwards

The wizard asks for it as step one, in plain words: a folder on your
own disk, and preferably empty. The card afterwards says which folder it
is and offers one way out - a different one, or none at all.

"None" is a real option and not a shrug. Without a working folder the
inbox still works, it just lives in Apollo's own storage; that is a
legitimate choice for someone who does not want their documents in a
folder of theirs, and the card says exactly that rather than implying
something is missing.
